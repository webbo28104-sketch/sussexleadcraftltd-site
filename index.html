About

Sussex Leadcraft Ltd

Based in Telscombe Cliffs, East Sussex, we are a specialist lead sheet contractor with experience across commercial, residential and heritage sectors. We operate across Greater London, the South East and South West.

We hold the highest achievable grading from the Lead Contractors Association, maintained through rigorous annual on-site inspection of workmanship and design. All installations comply with BS 6915 and Lead Sheet Association recommendations.

We work directly with main contractors, architects, quantity surveyors and building owners. We are experienced in the specific requirements of listed and historic buildings, ecclesiastical projects and large-scale commercial contracts.

  • General leadwork — flashings, valleys, gutters, dormers
  • New build roofing and cladding
  • Heritage and refurbishment projects
  • Sand cast lead supply and fix
  • Ornamental and architectural leadwork
  • Design advice and contract drawings

Services

Scope of works

We undertake the full range of specialist lead sheet work across commercial, residential and heritage sectors.

01

General Leadwork

Flashings, valleys, box gutters, parapet gutters, dormers and flat roofs. All work carried out to LCA standards using approved materials and fixing methods.

02

New Build Roofing & Cladding

Lead sheet roofing and cladding for new build schemes. We work with main contractors and developers from specification through to completion.

03

Heritage & Refurbishment

Specialist works to listed and historic buildings including churches, manor houses and civic structures. We understand conservation requirements and sensitive structures.

04

Sand Cast Supply & Fix

Traditional sand cast lead supplied and installed for high-specification heritage and ecclesiastical projects requiring period-authentic materials.

05

Ornamental Leadwork

Bespoke decorative lead features including hoppers, finials, downpipes and architectural detailing. Fabricated and installed by our specialist team.

06

Design & Drawing Services

Pre-contract design advice and production of contract drawings to support project specification, planning and contractor liaison.

The Lead Contractors Association was established in 1984 to promote quality standards in leadwork. Members are subject to annual vetting which results in a published grading.

The LCA's 25-year guarantee scheme was the first workmanship guarantee of its kind in the construction industry. Each registered project is independently inspected on completion.

Sussex Leadcraft holds the highest achievable annual grade. Our membership can be verified at leadcontractors.co.uk.
